# Elche CF

## Injuries
- **Josan Ferrández**: Suffered a potential hamstring injury during a friendly against Almería, leaving the match in the first half. His availability for the upcoming league match is uncertain.
- **Adam Boayar**: Also left the match against Almería due to ongoing issues, having only played for about half an hour.

## Transfers
- **Fede Redondo**: Officially signed from Inter Miami on a contract until 2030. The deal reportedly cost around €2 million for 50% of his rights. Redondo is a 22-year-old midfielder and son of the legendary Fernando Redondo.
- **Víctor Chust**: Joined on loan from Cádiz with an option to buy. The 25-year-old central defender has experience in both La Liga and Segunda División.
- **Martim Neto**: Signed from Benfica until 2028, adding depth to the midfield.
- **Álvaro Rodríguez**: Acquired from Real Madrid, he has signed a contract until 2029.
- **Germán Valera**: Joined from Valencia, also signed until 2027.
- **Alejandro Iturbe**: Goalkeeper signed from Atlético Madrid.
- **Bakary Traoré**: Renewed his contract until 2027 and was loaned to Tarazona.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match**: Elche CF vs. Real Betis
- **Date**: August 18, 2025
- **Venue**: Martínez Valero
- **Context**: Elche aims to break a 52-year streak without a victory in their opening match of the league. The last win was against Barcelona in 1973.
- **Quote**: Eder Sarabia has emphasized the need for additional signings, stating, "We need between four and six important signings."

## Match Reports
- **Elche 1-0 Almería**: A recent friendly where Álvaro Rodríguez scored the only goal, with Alejandro Iturbe making crucial saves to secure the win.
- **Elche 3-4 Al-Ain**: A pre-season match where Elche lost in a high-scoring affair, with goals from Mendoza, Mourad, and Rodríguez not enough to secure a draw.
- **Elche 2-1 Getafe**: In this friendly, Rodríguez scored on his debut, contributing to a solid performance from the team.

## Other Updates
- **Fan Engagement**: Elche has surpassed 27,000 season ticket holders, marking a record for the club.
- **Sponsorship**: VegaFibra has renewed its sponsorship with Elche for another season, continuing its support for the club's ambitions in La Liga.
