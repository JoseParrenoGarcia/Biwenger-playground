# Levante UD

## Injuries
- **Carlos Álvarez**: Suffering from pubalgia, he is unlikely to be fit for the opening match against Deportivo Alavés on August 16.
- **Matías Moreno**: Sustained a rectus femoris injury, initially expected to recover in about three weeks but remains a doubt for the start of the season.
- **Kervin Arriaga** and **Jon Ander Olasagasti**: Both have the same injury (rectus femoris) and are also doubtful for the first match.
- **Alfonso Pastor**: Recently underwent surgery for a fractured finger and is expected to be out for about four weeks.

## Transfers
- **Goduine Koyalipou**: Joined on loan from RC Lens with an option to buy. He is seen as a key addition to the attack.
- **Jon Ander Olasagasti**: Signed from Real Sociedad, he is expected to bolster the midfield.
- **Kervin Arriaga**: Another summer signing from Partizan, adding depth to the squad.
- **Alan Matturro**, **Jeremy Toljan**, **Victor García**, and **Manu Sánchez**: All have joined the club this summer, enhancing the defensive lineup.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match against Deportivo Alavés**: Scheduled for August 16 at 17:00 (local time). This will be Levante's first match back in La Liga after a four-season absence.

## Match Reports
- **Levante 0 - 2 CD Castellón**: This was Levante's first defeat of the pre-season, with Nick Markanich scoring both goals for Castellón. The team struggled to convert their chances despite starting strong.
- **Levante 2 - 0 Auxerre**: In their last pre-season match, Levante secured a victory with goals from Morales (penalty) and Lozano. The team showcased solid defensive capabilities, finishing the pre-season with five clean sheets and only two goals conceded in total.

## Additional Notes
- **Paco Cortés**: Recently renewed his contract until 2028 and has shown promise during pre-season, being considered a key player for the upcoming season.
- **Emily Lima**: Newly appointed coach for the women's team, emphasizing the importance of youth development and competitiveness.

## Summary
Levante UD has had a mixed pre-season with strong defensive performances but has faced injury challenges. They have made several key signings to bolster their squad ahead of their return to La Liga. The upcoming match against Alavés will be crucial for setting the tone for the season.
