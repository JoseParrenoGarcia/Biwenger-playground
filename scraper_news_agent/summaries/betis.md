# Real Betis

## Injuries
- **Diego Llorente**: Recovering from a serious hamstring injury; expected to be out for about four months post-surgery.
- **Marc Roca**: Recently underwent surgery on his left ankle; no estimated return date provided, but he will not participate in the pre-season.
- **Ez Abde**: Injured during the Conference League final; currently recovering and will not join the pre-season training in Portugal.

## Transfers
- **William Carvalho**: Negotiating a release from his contract with the Betis; expected to join Al Wakrah in Qatar. He has been a key player for the club but is no longer in the team's plans.
- **Borja Iglesias**: Back from a loan at Celta de Vigo; his future is uncertain as he prefers to join Celta permanently, but negotiations are ongoing.
- **Bruno Klimek**: A 17-year-old Polish goalkeeper has signed with Betis from Mallorca until 2027.
- **Guilherme Fernandes**: Officially loaned to Real Valladolid with an option to buy; Betis retains a buy-back clause.
- **Rodrigo Riquelme**: Officially signed from Atlético Madrid; the deal involves a 50% ownership agreement.
- **Nobel Mendy**: His transfer to PSV Eindhoven has been delayed due to bureaucratic issues; he is currently training in Sevilla.
- **Kristjan Asllani**: The Betis is in negotiations to acquire the 23-year-old midfielder from Inter Milan as a replacement for Johnny Cardoso.
- **Juan Ignacio Nardoni and Gabriel Moscardó**: Both are being considered as potential signings to strengthen the midfield.
