## Transfers
- **Pathé Ciss**: The Rayo Vallecano is upset with Celta de Vigo for contacting the player without prior notice. Ciss has a release clause of €10 million, and the club considers him a key player. 
- **Luiz Felipe**: Officially signed as the first summer reinforcement for the 2025/2026 season after leaving Olympique de Marseille. He has signed for one year.
- **Óscar Trejo**: Renewed his contract with Rayo Vallecano until June 2026, marking his tenth season with the club.
- **Raúl de Tomás**: Close to transferring to Al Wakrah in Qatar after not fitting into Iñigo Pérez's plans. He has a contract with Rayo until 2027 but is expected to leave soon.
- **Andrei Ratiu**: Started pre-season training with Rayo despite interest from clubs like Barcelona and Roma. His release clause is set at €25 million.
- **Juan Cruz**: Rayo Vallecano is interested in signing him, with a release clause of €10 million. Other clubs like Mallorca and Celta are also pursuing him.
- **Saúl Ñíguez**: In talks for a potential transfer to Rayo Vallecano from Atlético Madrid, but his high salary poses a challenge.
