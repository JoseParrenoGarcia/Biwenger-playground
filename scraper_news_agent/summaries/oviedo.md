# Real Oviedo

## Injuries
- **Haissem Hassan**: Suffered muscular discomfort during a friendly match against Deportivo, but has since returned to training and is expected to be available for the upcoming match against Villarreal.
- **Santiago Colombatto**: Out due to injury and has not participated in training.
- **Lucas Ahijado**: Out due to a muscular injury.
- **David Costas**: Suspended for the first match due to a red card from the previous season.
- **Álvaro Lemos**: Out with a torn anterior cruciate ligament, expected to miss several matches.
- **Jaime Seoane**: Also dealing with physical discomfort.

## Transfers
- **Ovie Ejaria**: Officially signed for two seasons after a successful trial period. His registration is pending but he is expected to be available for the match against Villarreal.
- **Santiago Londoño**: A 17-year-old Colombian striker has been signed but will remain at Envigado until the end of the year.
- **Interest in Marko Grujić**: The club is negotiating for the Serbian midfielder from Oporto, but the deal is complicated by financial constraints.
- **Interest in Munir El Haddadi**: The club is pursuing the former Leganés player as a potential signing after missing out on Jovic and Maksimovic.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match**: Villarreal vs Real Oviedo
- **Date**: August 15, 2025
- **Time**: 21:30 (local time)
- **Venue**: La Cerámica
- **Context**: This match marks Oviedo's return to La Liga after 24 years. Coach Veljko Paunovic emphasizes the team's readiness despite ongoing transfer activities and player injuries. He aims for a competitive performance against a strong Villarreal side.
- **Key Players**: Santi Cazorla will be honored during the match, having previously played for Villarreal.

## Match Reports
- **Leganés vs Real Oviedo (Preseason Friendly)**: The match ended in a 0-0 draw, highlighting the need for improvement in the attacking phase. Both teams had opportunities, but neither could capitalize, with notable performances from players like Santi Cazorla in the latter stages.

## Additional Notes
- The club is actively seeking to finalize additional signings before the transfer window closes, focusing on strengthening the defense and midfield.
- The atmosphere around the team is positive, with players expressing excitement about the upcoming season and the challenges ahead.
