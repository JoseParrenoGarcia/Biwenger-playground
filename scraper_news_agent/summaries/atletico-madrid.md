# Atlético Madrid

## Injuries
No major updates.

## Transfers
- **Ángel Correa** has officially left Atlético Madrid to join **Tigres UANL** for approximately **€10-11 million**. He expressed his desire for a new challenge and a more prominent role, stating, "Llevaba muchos años en el Atlético, donde fui feliz, pero me voy tranquilo porque lo dejé todo y era un momento para dar un cambio."
  
- **Rodrigo De Paul** is in advanced negotiations with **Inter Miami**. The Argentine midfielder has reportedly agreed on personal terms but the clubs are still finalizing the transfer fee, which Atlético expects to be between **€12-15 million**.

- **Cristian "Cuti" Romero** was a priority for Atlético, but the club has decided to withdraw from negotiations due to Tottenham's refusal to sell. The Spurs demand **€70 million**, which Atlético is not willing to pay.

- **Álex Baena** has joined Atlético from **Villarreal** for **€42 million** plus **€13 million** in variables. He is expected to significantly impact the team's midfield.

- **Matteo Ruggeri** has been signed from **Atalanta** for **€17 million**. He is seen as a versatile addition to the defense.

- **Rodrigo Riquelme** has been transferred to **Real Betis** for **€15.5 million**.

- **Saúl Ñíguez** is in talks with **Rayo Vallecano** for a potential move, but his high salary complicates the situation.
