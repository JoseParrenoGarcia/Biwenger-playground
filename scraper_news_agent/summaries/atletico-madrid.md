# Atlético Madrid

## Injuries
- **Pablo Barrios**: Returned to training after a low-grade muscle injury that kept him out of several pre-season matches. Expected to be available for the LaLiga opener against Espanyol.
- **Johnny Cardoso**: Missed training due to a knock received in a previous session. His availability for the match against Espanyol is uncertain.
- **Jose María Giménez**: Still recovering from a muscle injury sustained during the World Club Cup and is not expected to play in the upcoming match.
- **Alexander Sorloth**: Suffered a muscle strain and did not train recently. His participation in the match against Espanyol is also in doubt.

## Transfers
- **Giacomo Raspadori**: Officially signed from Napoli for €21 million plus €4 million in variables. He has signed a contract until 2030 and is expected to strengthen the attacking options.
- **Thomas Lemar**: Loaned to Girona FC for the current season.
- **Santiago Mouriño**: Re-purchased from Deportivo Alavés for €4 million and subsequently sold to Villarreal for €10 million.
- **Conor Gallagher**: His future is uncertain as he has not secured a regular spot in the starting lineup and could be a candidate for transfer if offers arise.
- **Nico González**: The club is reportedly in talks with Juventus for his transfer to bolster the attack.
