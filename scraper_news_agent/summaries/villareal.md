## Transfers
- **Thierno Barry** has officially transferred to **Everton** for **€32.5 million** plus **€5.5 million** in variables. The deal also includes a **10% sell-on clause** for future transfers. Barry expressed gratitude to Villarreal and its fans in a heartfelt farewell on social media, stating, "It has been an immense honor to wear this shirt."
  
- **Álex Baena** has joined **Atlético de Madrid** for approximately **€42 million** plus variables. He announced his departure with a promise to return to Villarreal in the future.

- **Rafa Marín** has been signed on loan from **Napoli** with an option to buy for **€15 million**. This move aims to strengthen Villarreal's defense following the departures of Eric Bailly and Raúl Albiol.

- **Ramón Terrats** has been loaned to **Espanyol** with an option to buy after a lack of playing time at Villarreal.

- **Kiko Femenía** has left the club after not renewing his contract, leaving Villarreal without an experienced right-back.

- Villarreal is reportedly interested in **Axel Disasi** (Chelsea) and **Joaquín Panichelli** (Alavés) but faces challenges due to their high price tags.
