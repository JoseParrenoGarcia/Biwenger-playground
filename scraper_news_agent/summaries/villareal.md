# Villarreal CF

## Injuries
- **Logan Costa**: Out for the season due to a torn ACL.
- **Willy Kambwala**: Significant injury, expected to miss several months.
- **Ayoze Pérez**: Suffering from adductor issues; doubtful for the match against Oviedo but may return for the next game.
- **Ilias Akhomach**: Recently received medical clearance after a long recovery from a torn ACL.

## Transfers
- **Pape Gueye**: Interest from NEOM SC in Saudi Arabia; Villarreal is currently calm as they have not received an official offer. He has a contract until 2028.
- **Denis Suárez**: Negotiating a move to Samsunspor in Turkey; expected to finalize soon.
- **Konstantinos Mavropanos**: Villarreal is negotiating with West Ham for the central defender, who is valued at €20 million.
- **Carlos Soler**: The club has decided not to pursue his signing after the arrival of Thomas Partey.
- **Thomas Partey**: Recently signed; his situation is controversial due to ongoing legal issues.
