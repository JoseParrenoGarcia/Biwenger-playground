# Osasuna

## Injuries
- **Iker Benito**: Suffered a ligament injury in his left ankle during a match against Real Sociedad. He will be out for approximately 4 to 6 weeks, missing the start of the league season against Real Madrid on August 19. 
- **Rubén García**: Experiencing muscle discomfort and did not participate in recent training sessions.
- **Víctor Muñoz**: Initially trained with reduced intensity but has returned to full training and is expected to be available for the match against Real Madrid.

## Transfers
- **Sergio Herrera**: Renewed his contract with Osasuna until 2028. His release clause increased from €14 million to €20 million. 
- **Jesús Areso**: Transferred to Athletic Club for €12 million after the club activated his release clause. He expressed gratitude towards Osasuna in his farewell message.
- **Valentín Rosier**: Signed for three seasons from Leganés.
- **Víctor Muñoz**: Joined Osasuna for five seasons from Real Madrid Castilla.
- **Jeremiah St. Juste**: Osasuna has reached a preliminary agreement with Sporting Lisbon for a transfer fee of around €4 million.
- **César Azpilicueta**: Under consideration for a return to Osasuna as a free agent after leaving Atlético Madrid.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match Against Real Madrid**: Scheduled for August 19, 2025, at Santiago Bernabéu. This will be the first match of the LaLiga EA Sports season. Coach Alessio Lisci is optimistic despite the short preparation time. Osasuna aims to start the season strong after a mixed pre-season.

## Match Reports
- **Friburgo 2 - 2 Osasuna**: In their final pre-season match, Osasuna managed to equalize after trailing, with goals from Aimar Oroz and Raúl García. The match ended with a late penalty awarded to Friburgo, resulting in a draw.
- **Osasuna 3 - 0 Mirandés**: Osasuna achieved their first victory of the pre-season, with Raúl García scoring twice and Budimir adding a third goal. The team showcased a strong performance under new coach Alessio Lisci.
- **Osasuna 0 - 2 Huesca**: In a pre-season match, Osasuna lost to Huesca, with Iker Benito scoring an own goal and Kortajarena sealing the victory for Huesca late in the game.

## Other Updates
- **Aimar Oroz**: Has returned to training after recovering from minor injuries and is expected to play a key role in the upcoming season.
- **Alessio Lisci**: Expressed satisfaction with the team's progress during pre-season but emphasized the need for continued improvement.
