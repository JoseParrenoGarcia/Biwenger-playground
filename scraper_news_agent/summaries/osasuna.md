# Osasuna

## Injuries
- **Jon Moncayola**: Suffered a hamstring injury in his right leg during the match against Real Valladolid on April 22, 2025. He will begin his recovery process and is pending evolution.

## Transfers
- **Valentin Rosier**: Signed for three seasons (until 2028) from Leganés. He will have a buyout clause of €15 million.
- **Unai García**: Will not continue with Osasuna after the 2024-25 season. He will say goodbye to fans in the match against Espanyol.
- **Víctor Muñoz**: Osasuna has reached an agreement with Real Madrid for his transfer for around €6 million for 50% of his rights. He is currently participating in the Club World Cup.
- **Ibaider Gárriz**: Left Osasuna Promesas to join Lugo.
- **Pablo Ibáñez**: Transferred to Deportivo Alavés until 2030.
- **Rubén Peña**: Returned to Leganés after leaving Osasuna.
- **Ante Budimir**: No contact from Real Madrid, but his future remains uncertain due to interest from various clubs.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Osasuna vs. Real Madrid**: Scheduled for August 19, 2025, at 21:00. This match will be the season opener for Osasuna and is expected to be challenging as it is against a strong opponent.

## Match Reports
- **Osasuna 2 - 0 Espanyol**: Osasuna secured a vital win, with goals from Ante Budimir and Raúl García. This victory keeps their hopes for European qualification alive.
- **Osasuna 4 - 2 Villarreal**: Osasuna lost after a poor first half, where they conceded three goals. They managed to score two in the second half but could not secure a comeback.
