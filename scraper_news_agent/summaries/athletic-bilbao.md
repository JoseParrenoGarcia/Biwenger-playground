# Athletic Bilbao

## Injuries
- **Unai Gómez**: Suffered a muscular injury in the rectus femoris of his right leg during the match against Arsenal. Expected to miss the opening match of LaLiga.
- **Oihan Sancet**: Sustained a ligament injury in his right knee during the match against Liverpool. Estimated recovery time is around two months.
- **Unai Egiluz**: Underwent successful surgery for a torn anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee. Expected to be out for 6 to 8 months.
- **Beñat Prados**: Has a moderate muscle injury in the left rectus femoris. His availability for the start of LaLiga is uncertain.
- **Aitor Paredes**: Experiencing a muscular overload in the left rectus femoris, but is expected to be available for the opening match against Sevilla.
- **Yeray Álvarez**: Currently suspended due to a positive doping test.

## Transfers
- **Asier Villalibre**: Transferred on loan to Racing de Santander from Deportivo Alavés for the season.
- **Peio Canales**: Close to a loan move to FC Andorra as part of a strategy to reduce the squad size.
- **Aymeric Laporte**: The Athletic is pursuing his signing from Al Nassr, but complications have arisen due to his injury and the need for a replacement at the Saudi club.
- **Julen Agirrezabala**: Loaned to Valencia for the upcoming season with an option for the club to buy.
- **Hugo Rincón**: Loaned to Girona for the 2025-26 season.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match**: Athletic Bilbao vs. Sevilla
- **Date**: August 17, 2025
- **Context**: Opening match of LaLiga EA Sports. The Athletic is looking to recover from a poor pre-season marked by injuries and defeats.

## Match Reports
- **Arsenal 3-0 Athletic**: The Athletic suffered its sixth consecutive defeat in pre-season. Goals were scored by Viktor Gyökeres, Bukayo Saka, and Kai Havertz. Unai Gómez was substituted early due to a muscular injury, adding to the team's injury woes. The match included a penalty shootout that Arsenal won 6-5, although it had no bearing on the match outcome.

## Additional Notes
- **Ernesto Valverde** expressed concern over the team's performance and the accumulation of injuries, stating, "It has been a tough pre-season for us. The results have not gone well, and we need to prepare for LaLiga."
