# Athletic Bilbao

## Injuries
- **Unai Egiluz**: Suffered a torn anterior cruciate ligament in his right knee during a friendly against Racing de Santander. He will be out for approximately 6 to 8 months after successful surgery.
- **Oihan Sancet**: Sustained a lateral ligament injury in his right knee during a match against Liverpool. His return is pending further evaluation.
- **Unai Gómez**: Injured his right rectus femoris muscle during the Arsenal match and is expected to miss the opening match of LaLiga.
- **Beñat Prados**: Also has a muscle injury in the rectus femoris of his left leg, with a moderate diagnosis.
- **Yeray Álvarez**: Suspended provisionally by UEFA due to a positive doping test.
- **Aitor Paredes**: Recently returned to training after recovering from a rectus femoris overload.

## Transfers
- **Iñigo Martínez**: Recently transferred to Al-Nassr and expressed a desire for a better farewell from Athletic.
- **Adu Ares**: Close to being loaned to Eibar for the season.
- **Peio Canales**: In discussions for a loan to Racing de Santander, with interest from Andorra as well.
- **Jesús Areso**: Signed from Osasuna for €12 million, with a contract until 2031.
- **Nico Williams**: Renewed his contract with Athletic until 2035, rejecting Barcelona's advances.
- **Aymeric Laporte**: Negotiations for his transfer from Al-Nassr are ongoing, with complications regarding his contract and salary.

## Previews of Upcoming Matches
- **Match**: Athletic Bilbao vs Sevilla FC  
  **Date**: August 17, 2025  
  **Time**: 19:30 (local)  
  **Context**: This match marks the opening of LaLiga for the Athletic, who are looking to break a six-year winless streak in season openers. Sevilla arrives with several injuries and a weakened squad.

## Match Reports
- **Friendly Results**: Athletic lost to Liverpool in two matches (4-1 and 3-2). The team struggled defensively and suffered multiple injuries during these games.
- **Performance Concerns**: The pre-season has been disappointing, with six losses in seven matches, raising concerns about the team's readiness for the upcoming season.

## Additional Notes
- **Laporte's Situation**: The Athletic is keen on securing Laporte's transfer, which has been complicated by his current contract situation with Al-Nassr.
- **Community Impact**: The club is mourning the loss of Juan Antonio Virumbrales, the club's number one member, who passed away at the age of 99.
